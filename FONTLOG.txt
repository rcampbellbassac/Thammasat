FONTLOG for Thammasat

Thammasat (ທຳມະຊາດ) is a display font for Lao, Thai, Khmer and Latin drawn from a hand-painted
sign photographed in Laos in 2019. Friendly painted park-sign lettering: round heads, generous loops, even stroke. The bilingual Latin on the sign (rounded single-storey a, looped l) is a candidate companion for later.

How it was made
- Lao key letters (ແ ງ ທ ່ ອ ຽ ວ ໍ ມ ະ ຊ າ ດ ຸ ິ ຍ ນ ກ ຕ ີ) were traced from the photograph (scripts/segment.py, scripts/trace.py).
- The other letters were generated on the Noto designs with a simulated brush whose size and the font's width were
  measured from the traced letters (scripts/auto.py), then merged with the traced letters (scripts/hybrid.py).
- Generated letters are drafts to be redrawn toward the traced ones before 1.0.
